📝 Sindhi Grammar Basics

🔤 Sentence Structure

Sindhi follows a Subject-Object-Verb (SOV) word order, similar to Hindi and Urdu.

Example:

I eat food. آئون کاڌو کائيندو آهيان (Aoon khadho khaayindo aahiyan)

👤 Personal Pronouns

I آئون (Aoon)
You توهان (Tawhaan)
He/She هو (Hu)
We اسان (Asaan)
They هن (Hun)

🔄 Common Verbs

To be هجڻ (Hujan)
To go وڃڻ (Wajan)
To come اچڻ (Achhan)
To eat کائڻ (Khaayaan)
To drink پيئڻ (Peeyaan)

🏡 Daily Good Habits at Home

🙏 Respect Elders

Always greet elders with respect and listen to their advice

هميشه وڏن کي سلام ڪريو ۽ انهن جي مشورن تي ڌيان ڏيو

(Hamesha wadan khe salaam kayo ain unhan je mashwaran te dhyaan diyo)

🧹 Keep Clean

Keep your room and house clean and tidy

پنهنجو ڪمرو ۽ گهر صاف ستھرو رکو

(Pahnjo kamro ain ghar saaf suthro rakho)

📚 Study Daily

Make time to study and read every day

هر روز پڙهڻ ۽ سکڻ لاءِ وقت ڪڍو

(Har roz parhan ain sikhan laae waqt kadho)

🍎 Eat Healthy

Eat fruits, vegetables and balanced meals

ميوا، سائي ڀاڄي ۽ متوازن کاڌو کائو

(Mewa, sai bhaji ain mutawazan khadho khao)

💤 Sleep Well

Go to bed early and get enough sleep

جلدي سمهو ۽ پورو آرام ڪريو

(Jaldi sumho ain puro aram karyo)

🤲 Be Thankful

Say thank you and appreciate what you have

شُڪر ادا ڪريو ۽ جيڪي توهان وٽ آهي ان جي قدر ڪريو

(Shukriyo ada karyo ain jeki tawhan wat aahe un ji qadar karyo)

🎓 Fascinating Facts About Sindhi

Discover amazing and uncommon facts about the Sindhi language and culture!

🔤
Sindhi has 52 letters in its alphabet.
سنڌي ٻولي ۾ 52 اکر آهن.
(Sindhi boli mein 52 akhar ahan.)
�️
Sindhi is named after the Sindhu (Indus) River.
سنڌي ٻولي جو نالو سنڌو نديء تي رکيو ويو آهي.
(Sindhi boli jo naalo Sindhu nadi te rakhyo wayo aahe.)
👕
Ajrak is a colorful cloth with special patterns worn by Sindhi people.
اجرڪ هڪ رنگين ڪپڙو آهي جيڪو سنڌي ماڻهو پائيندا آهن.
(Ajrak hik rangeen kapro aahe jeko Sindhi manho paindan aahan.)
🧠
Sindhi is written from right to left.
سنڌي ساڄي کان کاٻي طرف لکبي آهي.
(Sindhi saje khan khabe taraf likbi aahe.)
�
Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai is the most famous Sindhi poet.
شاه عبداللطيف ڀٽائي سنڌي ٻولي جو سڀ کان مشهور شاعر آهي.
(Shah Abdul Latif Bhitai Sindhi boli jo sabh khan mashhoor shaair aahe.)
�
Sindhi music uses special drums called "dholak".
سنڌي موسيقيء ۾ ڊهول ۽ ڊهولڪ استعمال ڪيا ويندا آهن.
(Sindhi musiqi mein dhol ain dholak istemal kya wenda aahan.)
�
Sindhi Biryani is a famous rice dish loved by many people.
سنڌي برياني هڪ مشهور کاڌو آهي جيڪو گهڻن ماڻهن کي پسند آهي.
(Sindhi biryani hik mashhoor khado aahe jeko ghanan manhan khe pasand aahe.)
👒
Sindhi men often wear a special cap called "Topi".
سنڌي مرد اڪثر هڪ خاص ٽوپي پائيندا آهن جنهن کي "سنڌي ٽوپي" چيو ويندو آهي.
(Sindhi mard aksar hik khas topi pahrenda aahan jinhan khe "topi" chayo wendo aahe.)
🤝
Saying "Salam" with your right hand near your forehead is a traditional Sindhi greeting.
پيشاني ويجهو ساڄو هٿ رکي "سلام" چوڻ سنڌي ثقافت جو روايتي طريقو آهي.
(Peshaani vijho saajo hath rakhi "Adaab" chawan Sindhi salaam jo rawayati tariqo aahe.)

📚 Famous Sindhi Poets

Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai

شاه عبدالطيف ڀٽائي

(Shah Abdul Lateef Bhittai)

Years: 1689-1752

Legacy: Greatest Sindhi poet, revered as a saint and mystic

Famous Work: "Shah Jo Risalo" - a collection of poems with 30 musical chapters called Surs

Philosophy: His poetry blends Islamic mysticism with local culture

شاه جو رسالو (شاه جو رسالو) - ٣٠ سرن تي مشتمل شاعري جو مجموعو

شاه سنڌ جو عظيم شاعر، صوفي ۽ فلسفي آهي، جنهن جي شاعري ۾ روحاني ۽ انساني قدر شامل آهن

(Shah Sindh jo azeem shair, sufi ain falsafi aahe, jinhan ji shairi mein roohani ain insaani qadar shamil aahan)

Sachal Sarmast

سچل سرمست

(Sachal Sarmast)

Years: 1739-1829

Legacy: Revolutionary Sufi poet and mystic known for challenging orthodoxy

Famous Work: "Diwan-e-Sachal" - featuring poems in multiple languages

Philosophy: Promoted religious harmony and universal brotherhood

ديوان سچل (ديوان سچل) - متعدد زبانن ۾ شاعري جو مجموعو

سچل هڪ انقلابي صوفي شاعر هو، جنهن سڀني مذهبن ۾ وحدت جو پيغام ڏنو

(Sachal hik inqilabi sufi shair ho, jinhan sabni mazahiban mein wahdat jo paigham dino)

Sheikh Ayaz

شيخ اياز

(Sheikh Ayaaz)

Years: 1923-1997

Legacy: Most significant modern Sindhi poet and progressive writer

Famous Work: "Sindhu" and "Bhounr Bhare Akas"

Philosophy: Promoted social reform, cultural identity, and women's rights

سنڌو (سنڌو) ۽ ڀونر ڀري آڪاس (ڀونر ڀري آڪاس)

شيخ اياز سنڌي ادب جو انقلابي شاعر جيڪو سماجي عدل ۽ ثقافتي شناخت جي لاءِ وڏي طور لکندو هو

(Sheikh Ayaz Sindhi adab jo inqilabi shair jeko samaji adal ain saqafati shanakhat ji laee waddi tor te likhando ho)

Ustad Bukhari

استاد بخاري

(Ustaad Bukhaari)

Years: 1841-1924

Legacy: Classical poet, scholar and spiritual teacher

Famous Work: "Kashful Abyat" - collection of religious and mystical poetry

Philosophy: Combined Islamic teachings with traditional Sindhi values

ڪشف الابيات (ڪشف الابيات) - مذهبي ۽ روحاني شاعري جو مجموعو

استاد بخاري عالم، شاعر ۽ روحاني رهبر هو جنهن جي شاعريءَ ۾ اسلامي تعليمات ۽ صوفياڻو فلسفو موجود آهي

(Ustad Bukhari aalim, shair ain roohani rehbar ho jinhan ji shairi mein Islami talimat ain sufiyano falsafo moujood aahe)

📖 Famous Literary Works

Shah Jo Risalo

شاه جو رسالو

(Shah Jo Risaalo)

The most renowned collection of Sindhi poetry by Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai, organized into 30 chapters called "Sur"

Time Period: Early 18th century

Significance: Considered the pinnacle of Sindhi literature and spiritual wisdom

هي ڪتاب سنڌي ادب جو عظيم شاهڪار آهي، ۽ ان ۾ ٽيهه سر شامل آهن جيڪي روحاني ۽ ثقافتي ڳالهيون بيان ڪن ٿا

(He kitaab Sindhi adab jo azeem shahkar aahe, ain un mein tihe sur shamil aahan jeke roohani ain saqafati galhiyoon bayan kan tha)

Divan-e-Sachal

ديوان سچل

(Deewan-e-Sachal)

Comprehensive poetry collection of Sachal Sarmast, featuring mystical verses in multiple languages including Sindhi, Persian, and Urdu

Time Period: Late 18th/early 19th century

Significance: Exemplifies religious harmony and universal love

سچل جي شاعري مذهبي يڪجهتي ۽ عالمي محبت جو پيغام ڏئي ٿي، هي ڪتاب سندس متعدد ٻولين ۾ لکيل شاعري جو مجموعو آهي

(Sachal ji shairi mazahabi yakjehti ain aalmi muhabbat jo paigham dai thi, he kitaab sandas mutadid boliyun mein likhyal shairi jo majmuo aahe)

🖋️ Famous Sindhi Poetry Verses

Explore these iconic verses from Sindhi's greatest poets

Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai

جي ڀلا ڪر، ته ڀلا ٿيندئي،
جي برا ڪر، ته برا ٿيندئي،
جيڪي ٻڄ پوکيندين، تنهنجي اگر،
سو ئي فصل لڻندين، مٿي نيندئي۔

(Je bhala kar, ta bhala thinda-e,
Je bura kar, ta bura thinda-e,
Jeke beej pokhanden, tahinji agar,
So-e fasal lananden, mathai ninda-e)

Translation: "If you do good, good will happen to you. If you do bad, bad will happen to you. Whatever seed you sow in your field, that is the crop you will harvest."

Sachal Sarmast

هر روپ ۾ يار منهنجو،
هر شئي ۾ ديدار منهنجو،
هر گل ۾ گلزار منهنجو،
هر ذرّي ۾ دلدار منهنجو۔

(Har roop me yaar munhinjo,
Har shai me deedar munhinjo,
Har gul me gulzar munhinjo,
Har zarree me dildar munhinjo)

Translation: "In every form is my beloved, in everything is my vision. In every flower is my garden, in every particle is my sweetheart."

Sheikh Ayaz

اسين ٻڏل ماڻهو آهيون
پر منهنجي خلوص تي نه وڃ
اسين ٽٽل تارا آهيون
چمڪي سگهون ٿا

(Aseen budal manu aahiyon
Par munhinji khuloos te na wanj
Aseen tutal tara aahiyon
Chamaki saghoon tha)

Translation: "We are drowning people, but don't be deceived by my sincerity. We are broken stars, yet we can still shine."
